Sample Approval Checklist for Custom Craft Supplies

A sample should be reviewed against written criteria, not memory. The approved reference becomes the basis for subsequent order communication.

Appearance and color

Review color direction, print clarity, glitter distribution, transparency, surface finish and any acceptable variation range.

Dimensions and physical details

Confirm length, width, thickness, hole position, flatback condition, edge finish and other product-specific details.

Assortment composition

For mixed packs, confirm the selected components, approximate ratios, excluded items and whether color balance is controlled or random.

Packaging presentation

Check pack weight or piece count, bag or jar size, seal, label placement, barcode readability, warning text and carton marking.

Document deviations

Record every requested correction in writing. Avoid approving a sample while separately expecting undocumented changes in bulk production.

Sample approval is an operational control. It does not replace destination-market compliance review or third-party laboratory testing where those are required.

Approval Record

  • Sample version and date
  • Product code or project reference
  • Approved dimensions
  • Color and finish reference
  • Assortment ratio
  • Packaging layout
  • Required corrections
  • Final written approval
