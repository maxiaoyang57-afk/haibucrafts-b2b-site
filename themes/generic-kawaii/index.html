Generic Kawaii sourcing questions

What does generic kawaii mean here?

An original cute style built from general visual qualities without using a protected character, logo or branded universe.

Can I request a famous-character lookalike?

No. Describe general attributes and develop a distinct original design.

Is private-label packaging possible?

Feasibility can be reviewed with artwork, barcode, warning text, pack format and destination requirements.

Original and non-branded by design

Use generic motifs and original artwork. Do not request protected characters, logos, team marks or copied patterns.
