Privacy Policy

How information is handled when you visit HAIBUCRAFT or send a wholesale inquiry.

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Information we collect

When you submit a wholesale inquiry, we collect the contact, company, product, quantity, delivery and customization information you provide. If reference images are enabled for an inquiry, compressed copies may be included with your submission.

Website analytics and cookie choices

We use Vercel Web Analytics for anonymous, cookie-free website statistics. We also use Google Analytics 4 with Consent Mode v2 to understand page visits, approximate location, device and browser characteristics, referral sources and buyer interactions such as product-link clicks, quotation-button clicks and completed inquiry submissions.

Google's analytics tag loads with analytics storage denied by default. Before you accept analytics, Google may receive limited cookieless consent and measurement pings and Analytics cookies are not used. If you select “Accept analytics,” analytics storage is updated to granted. Advertising storage, ad user data and ad personalization remain denied, and Google signals are disabled. We do not intentionally send names, email addresses, phone numbers, inquiry messages or other inquiry form fields to Google Analytics. You can change your choice through “Cookie choices” in the website footer at any time. Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

How we use inquiry information

Inquiry information is used to review product availability, quantities, packaging, customization, destination requirements and sales follow-up. We do not operate a retail checkout and do not sell submitted contact information.

WhatsApp communications

If you choose to contact us through WhatsApp, your phone number or account details, message content and related communication metadata may be processed by WhatsApp and Meta under their own terms and privacy practices. HAIBUCRAFT does not control that third-party processing. You may use the website inquiry form or email instead.

Service providers and retention

Vercel supports hosting and anonymous analytics, Google processes limited cookieless measurement signals when analytics storage is denied and consented analytics data when it is granted, and email-delivery providers process information needed to deliver inquiries. Records are retained only as long as reasonably needed for quotation, order support, security and legal obligations.

Your choices

You may accept or decline Google Analytics and change that choice through “Cookie choices” in the website footer. You may also ask us to correct or delete inquiry contact information when applicable. Operational, legal or security records may need to be retained for a limited period.

Contact

For privacy questions, email sales@haibucrafts.com.
