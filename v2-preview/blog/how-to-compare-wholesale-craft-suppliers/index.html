Supplier Review

How to Compare Wholesale Craft Suppliers

Useful supplier comparison goes beyond the lowest unit price and looks at how requirements are translated into controlled order steps.

Buyer guideUpdated August 2026HAIBUCRAFT editorial
01

Compare like-for-like quotations

Use the same product code, quantity unit, packing specification, destination and customization scope for every supplier response.

02

Evaluate sample and change control

Ask how samples are identified, revisions are recorded and approved differences are carried into production.

03

Review operational evidence

Look for real product imagery, facility or workflow evidence, clear quality checkpoints and realistic communication about limitations.

04

Read documents by scope

Check the company, product, sample and standard named in each test report, certificate or audit summary rather than counting document logos.

Supplier selection should reflect the specific order risk, destination and product use. No single checklist replaces buyer due diligence.
Working Checklist

Use this in your inquiry.

  • Comparable quotation
  • Product-code clarity
  • Sample revision control
  • Packaging capability
  • Document scope
  • Response quality
