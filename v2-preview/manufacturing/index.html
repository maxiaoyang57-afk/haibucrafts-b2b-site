Workflow

A defined path from buyer brief to shipment preparation.

1. Requirement Review

Confirm product type, reference design, target quantity, packaging and destination market.

2. Sample Development

Coordinate available styles, colors, custom components and sample approval.

3. Production Follow-up

Track approved requirements, timing checkpoints and batch progress.

4. Inventory Handling

Organize products by SKU, batch or order program for clearer picking.

5. Packing Preparation

Review unit packing, assortment ratios, labels and cartons.

6. Export Coordination

Support consolidation, shipment details and logistics handoff.
