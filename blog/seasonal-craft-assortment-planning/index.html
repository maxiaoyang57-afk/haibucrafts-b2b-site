Seasonal Craft Assortment Planning Guide

Seasonal programs need a reverse timeline that allows decisions to be approved before the selling window becomes the shipping deadline.

By HAIBUCRAFT Buyer Resources Scope reviewed by Product & Quality Coordination Last reviewed August 6, 2026

Internal sourcing guidance based on catalog, sample, packaging, production and document-review workflows. Review scope is explained in the editorial policy.

01

Start from the required arrival date

Work backward from the warehouse or retail launch date and include transport, inspection, packing, production, sampling and quotation time.

02

Freeze the assortment early

Confirm product codes, quantities, ratios and substitutes. Late assortment changes can affect pricing, packing and document scope.

03

Coordinate artwork and samples

Plan time for buyer files, sample production, revision comments and written approval before bulk packaging is ordered.

04

Protect the final shipping window

Define inspection, rework decision time, carton documents and booking responsibility. Keep contingency for issues that require buyer approval.

Lead time is order-specific. A seasonal plan should use confirmed quotation milestones rather than a general website estimate.
Working Checklist

Use this in your inquiry.

  • Arrival deadline
  • SKU assortment
  • Sample approval
  • Artwork freeze
  • Inspection window
  • Shipping responsibility
