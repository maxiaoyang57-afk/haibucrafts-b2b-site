Buyer FAQ

Holiday sourcing questions

How early should a holiday range be planned?

Allow time for quotation, samples, artwork, production, inspection and transport before the required arrival date.

Can holiday packs contain several materials?

Yes, but counts, weights, labeling and internal protection should be defined for each material.

Do you produce licensed holiday characters?

This sourcing strategy uses original, generic seasonal motifs only.

Original and non-branded by design

Use generic motifs and original artwork. Do not request protected characters, logos, team marks or copied patterns.
