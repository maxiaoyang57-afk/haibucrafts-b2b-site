Brand & Factory Relationship

A clear distinction between the HAIBUCRAFT brand and the legal manufacturing entity.

HAIBUCRAFT is presented as an independent B2B brand and sourcing interface. Manufacturing, assembly, packing, warehouse and related operational resources may be provided through the associated factory organization and approved supply resources.

Because the legal company name shown in factory records is different from the HAIBUCRAFT brand name, the website avoids implying that the brand name itself is the registered factory entity.

Transparency Principles

Claims are limited to information that can be supported.

  • No unsupported production-capacity claims.
  • No blanket certification claim across every SKU.
  • No fixed MOQ or lead-time promise applied to all products.
  • Safety and testing requirements are reviewed by exact SKU, intended use and destination market.
  • Factory ownership and legal-entity wording remain distinct from the HAIBUCRAFT brand.
Important: decorative craft components are not edible. Many items are small parts and may not be suitable for children under 3. Final warnings, age grading and compliance documents must be reviewed against the exact product and market.

Company FAQ

Information buyers commonly need before starting.

Is HAIBUCRAFT a retail store?

No. The V2 structure is designed for B2B quotation, wholesale sourcing, custom development and private-label programs.

Does every product have the same MOQ and lead time?

No. These are confirmed by SKU, quantity, packaging format, customization and current production conditions.

Can buyers request several categories together?

Yes. A mixed inquiry can include multiple product codes, quantities and packaging requirements.

Are testing documents available for every item?

Documentation must be reviewed by exact SKU, intended use and destination market. A report for one item should not be assumed to cover the full catalog.

Prepare a sourcing brief for review.

Include product codes, target quantities, packaging, destination country and customization requirements.
