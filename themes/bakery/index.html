Buyer FAQ

Bakery sourcing questions

What belongs in a generic bakery theme?

Original cookies, cakes, pastries and decorating motifs can be used without referencing a branded property.

Can bakery and fruit designs be combined?

Yes, when size, palette and pack ratios are planned together.

How should miniature food pieces be labeled?

Present them as decorative, non-edible craft components with warnings appropriate to the product and market.

Original and non-branded by design

Use generic motifs and original artwork. Do not request protected characters, logos, team marks or copied patterns.
