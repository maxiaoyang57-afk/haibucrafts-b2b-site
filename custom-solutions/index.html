Responsibility Boundaries

Custom development is conditional, not automatic.

Feasibility depends on design detail, material behavior, dimensions, quantity, packaging and market requirements. A request is not treated as approved until the project scope and reference standard are confirmed.

  • Custom shape, pattern, color and artwork feasibility require review for the selected material.
  • Development fees, sample shipping, revision scope and tooling ownership or exclusivity apply only as agreed in writing.
  • MOQ and lead time are confirmed per project, not published as blanket promises.
  • Testing and documentation must match the exact SKU, material, project and destination market; visual QC is not safety certification.
  • Buyer-supplied artwork must be authorized for use.

Custom Project FAQ

Questions buyers commonly ask before sampling.

Do resin and polymer clay follow the same process?

They share brief review, sampling and written approval, but development differs. Resin may need shape modeling and new tooling. Polymer clay may need pattern or forming preparation; a 3D model or mold is not required for every design.

Does approving a digital proof approve the product?

No. A drawing or render confirms design intent. A physical sample is needed to review the actual material, scale, color, finish and intended application before written sample approval.

Does sample approval start bulk production?

No. Bulk quantity, pricing, MOQ, payment, timing, QC and packing requirements are confirmed separately. Tooling rights and exclusivity are determined only by the agreed terms.

Can you make a product from our artwork?

Feasibility is reviewed against shape, detail, dimensions, color count, material and quantity. Send editable artwork or a clear reference image.

Can several categories be packed together?

Mixed-category assortments can be reviewed when component compatibility, pack ratio and total pack weight are defined.

Can you use our label and barcode?

Private-label packaging may support buyer artwork, barcode and warning text after file and layout review.

Do custom products automatically have testing reports?

No. Testing scope must be confirmed for the exact product, intended use and destination market.
