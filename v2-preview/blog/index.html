Editorial Principles

Useful guidance without unsupported guarantees.

Each guide separates operational checks from legal compliance, certification and laboratory testing.

  • No universal MOQ or lead-time promises
  • No blanket certification statements
  • No substitution of inspection for testing
  • Product- and market-specific confirmation
