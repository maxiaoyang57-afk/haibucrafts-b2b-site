01

Clean the case and try the layout

Remove the phone and wipe the outside of the case clean and dry. Place the three larger shapes first, then try the smaller clay pieces in the gaps. Keep the camera and rim clear.

02

Glue, press and cure

Put a small dot of compatible adhesive on each flat back. Use tweezers for thin slices and press each piece gently into place. Keep the case flat until the adhesive has fully cured according to its label.

03

Fit the optional strap

Pass the cord loop through the case’s lanyard holes. Feed the strand through the loop and gently tighten it.

Assembly and finished look are illustrated. The mini clay starfish are a styling example. Ask about a matching sample. The case, adhesive and blue beaded strap are separate supplies.
