Packaging and Quality Checkpoints for B2B Orders

Clear checkpoints help buyers separate product quality, packout accuracy and destination-market documentation into manageable controls.

Product identity

Confirm the product code, approved reference, color or theme, selected components and any buyer-specific variation before packout begins.

Count or weight control

State whether each unit is controlled by piece count, net weight or assortment ratio. Define the allowed tolerance where appropriate.

Primary packaging

Check pouch, jar or container size, seal condition, label placement, barcode readability and product visibility.

Warnings and market text

Confirm the buyer-approved warning language, age grading, importer details and destination-market text. These requirements should be supplied or approved by the responsible buyer.

Outer cartons and shipment handover

Define units per carton, carton markings, gross and net weight records, carton condition and the documents needed for shipment coordination.

Packout inspection confirms conformance to the agreed order instructions. It does not by itself prove chemical, mechanical or regulatory compliance.

Packout Checklist

  • Correct SKU and approved reference
  • Piece count or net weight
  • Assortment ratio
  • Seal and container condition
  • Label and barcode placement
  • Warning and age text
  • Units per carton
  • Carton marks and handover records
