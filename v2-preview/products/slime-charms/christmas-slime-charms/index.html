Buyer-fit guidance

Plan a christmas assortment around real pack decisions.

Who this shortlist fits

For slime brands and DIY-kit buyers, the seven-code slime shortlist covers classic Santa, tree, snowflake, peppermint, snowman and gingerbread directions, with YX4109 shown separately as a related Christmas polymer clay option.

Mix ratios and pack units

Start with a written ratio by SKU or motif family (for example, focal charms versus small filler pieces). Confirm the final count or weight per selling unit against the approved sample.

Sampling and custom review

Request a representative sample of the selected codes before artwork and packing are approved. Note substitutions, color tolerance and whether every design must appear in each bag.

Reverse-plan from arrival

Work backward from the required arrival date: quotation and sample review, artwork approval, production, inspection, packing and transport each need an agreed decision window.
