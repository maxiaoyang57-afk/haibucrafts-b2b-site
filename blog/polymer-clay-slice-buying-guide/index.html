Polymer Clay Slice Buying Guide for Wholesale Buyers

A useful polymer-clay-slice inquiry describes both the visual theme and the measurable pack requirements.

By HAIBUCRAFT Buyer Resources Updated October 25, 2026

Sourcing guidance for a project-specific discussion. Confirm the applicable steps and terms in writing with HAIBU. See our editorial policy.

01

Define theme and intended use

State whether the slices are for slime, nail art, resin crafts, shaker fillers or DIY kits because size and pack format may differ.

02

Confirm dimensions and thickness

Use an approved sample or written measurement range. Do not rely on a photograph alone to establish scale.

03

Specify color and mix balance

For assorted designs, define required themes, excluded colors, target ratios and whether every pack needs the same composition.

04

Distinguish slices from sprinkle mixes

For a single motif, confirm slice dimensions and thickness. For bulk polymer clay sprinkles, also define motif and color ratios, excluded designs and whether each bag must contain every component. Net weight does not guarantee a fixed piece count.

05

Choose a packing unit

State grams, pieces, bags, jars or sets and include label, barcode, warning and carton requirements where applicable.

06

Buying for a liquid-filled shaker tumbler

Tell us whether the pieces will sit in a dry shaker or a liquid-filled wall. For liquid use, describe the exact water-glycerin or water-glue formulation, proportions, wall gap and planned storage conditions. A general shaker-filler application does not establish long-term liquid compatibility.

Liquid compatibility has not been established for the catalog examples below. Request any available SKU-specific evidence and approve samples in your actual formulation before a bulk order.

07

Agree what the sample trial must check

Confirm the selected SKU, component material, measured width and thickness, test liquid, observation period and conditions. Record color bleeding, swelling, softening, clumping and movement in the finished vessel. Discuss any required test method with the supplier; do not infer float or sink behavior from a product photograph.

08

Prepare a separate line for each sample and retail pack

List each design code and requested sample weight separately, then describe the retail grams per bag or jar, labels, mix proportions and destination. Ask for sample availability, packing feasibility, charges and shipping as separate quotation items rather than treating the source bulk pack as a sample offer.

Exact material, dimensions, MOQ, lead time and testing scope must be confirmed against the selected product code and approved sample.
Working Checklist

Use this in your inquiry.

  • Theme and use
  • Dimensions and thickness
  • Color range and mix ratio
  • Dry or liquid-filled application
  • Exact liquid formulation and sample checks
  • Sample weight per design
  • Retail pack unit and destination
