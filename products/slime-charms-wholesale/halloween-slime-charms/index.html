Who this shortlist fits

For seasonal slime drops, party kits and retail-ready DIY assortments, these six slime-only codes cover ghost, skeleton, candy-corn, pumpkin, cat and spider directions.

Mix ratios and pack units

Define the target share of focal charms and filler slices by selling unit. If a bag must contain each motif, state the minimum count per design rather than relying on an informal theme name.

Sampling and custom review

Approve a physical or photo sample for color, finish, size and motif balance before packaging artwork is released. Record any approved substitute in the SKU brief.

Reverse-plan from arrival

Work backward from the Halloween launch or warehouse arrival date and reserve time for quote, sample, artwork, production, inspection, packing and transport.
