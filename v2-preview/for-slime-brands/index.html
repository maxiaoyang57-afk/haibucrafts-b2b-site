Better RFQs

Send one brief that explains what the customer will actually receive.

A structured brief reduces back-and-forth and helps sales review stock, custom and packaging requirements together.

1. Finished use

Tell us whether the components are for slime jars, sensory kits, retail refill packs, subscription boxes or another format.

2. Product shortlist

Send the SKU codes you already like, plus reference images or a theme direction if the assortment is still open.

3. Quantity & packout

State the estimated quantity and unit, pack count or weight, mix ratios and any separate refill or retail-pack requirements.

4. Market requirements

Include destination country, target arrival date, labeling needs and any testing or documentation requirements that must be reviewed.

Slime Brand FAQ

Questions to settle before quotation and sample review.

Can a slime brand source several product categories in one project?

Mixed-category sourcing can be reviewed. Send the selected product codes, intended use, quantities, packing requirements and destination so compatibility and quotation scope can be confirmed.

Can we build a seasonal or color-led assortment?

Theme and assortment requests can be reviewed against the selected stock items or custom requirements. Share the target theme, colors, pack format and reference images.

Can HAIBUCRAFT support private-label packaging?

Private-label options may include buyer artwork, labels, barcodes and customized pack formats, subject to project review and confirmation.

How should a slime brand ask about testing or documentation?

Documentation requirements should be stated for the exact SKU, intended use and destination market. Testing scope is reviewed product by product rather than assumed for an entire category.
