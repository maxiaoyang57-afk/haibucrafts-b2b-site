Bulk sourcing guide

Specify sprinkle mixes by SKU, ratio and selling-unit format.

For a clearer wholesale quotation, separate product identity from pack design. First choose the exact sprinkle SKUs, then define quantity or mix ratio, bag/jar/kit format, label requirements and destination market.

  • Reference exact YX product codes.
  • State quantity per SKU or target assortment ratio.
  • Define slime-kit, shaker, craft-pack or private-label use.
  • Confirm bag, jar, label, barcode and carton requirements.
  • Confirm MOQ, sample scope, lead time and documentation in writing.

Approve the product and the pack separately

  • State sample quantity per SKU separately from the planned bulk or retail pack. Confirm sample availability, fees and shipping in the quotation.
  • Confirm all mix components, not just the clay motif: YX660 includes pearl accents and YX4019 uses 3D Christmas forms. Ask for the selected SKU's component materials, width and thickness before finalizing a kit or vessel.
  • Separate dry-shaker use from a liquid-filled tumbler project. This collection does not establish long-term liquid compatibility; approve samples in your exact formulation and storage conditions.

Buyer FAQ

Wholesale polymer clay sprinkles sourcing questions

Can polymer clay sprinkles be ordered in mixed-SKU assortments?

Yes. Provide selected product codes, target quantities or ratios, packing format and destination market for review.

What is the MOQ?

MOQ is confirmed by selected SKU, quantity, packing and customization requirements. There is no blanket MOQ for every sprinkle mix.

Can sprinkle mixes be packed for slime kits or private-label programs?

Yes. Packing format, labels, barcodes, warning text, assortment ratios and outer-carton requirements can be reviewed in the quotation.

Are the decorative sprinkles edible?

No. They are decorative craft components and must not be represented or used as food.

Can these sprinkles be used in a liquid-filled tumbler?

This collection does not establish long-term liquid compatibility. Confirm the selected SKU's materials, dimensions and any available test evidence, then approve samples in your exact formulation and storage conditions.
