Privacy Policy

How information is handled when you visit HAIBUCRAFT or send a wholesale inquiry.

Last updated: July 23, 2026

Information we collect

When you submit a wholesale inquiry, we collect the contact, company, product, quantity, delivery and customization information you provide. If reference images are enabled for an inquiry, compressed copies may be included with your submission.

Anonymous website analytics

We use Vercel Web Analytics to understand anonymous page views, referral sources, approximate country, device and browser information. This helps us improve buyer-facing content and inquiry workflows.

How we use inquiry information

Inquiry information is used to review product availability, quantities, packaging, customization, destination requirements and sales follow-up. We do not operate a retail checkout and do not sell submitted contact information.

Service providers and retention

Hosting, analytics and email-delivery providers may process limited information needed to operate the site and deliver inquiries. Records are retained only as long as reasonably needed for quotation, order support, security and legal obligations.

Your choices

You may ask us to correct or delete inquiry contact information when applicable. Operational, legal or security records may need to be retained for a limited period.
