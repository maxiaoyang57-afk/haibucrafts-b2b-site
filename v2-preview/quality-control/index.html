Inspection Process

Practical controls aligned with the approved buyer brief.

The final page will combine verified procedures with warehouse, sample-management and packing imagery. Each checkpoint is tied to visible product or order requirements.

Inspection scope varies by product, quantity, packing format and destination requirements. The website will not imply that every SKU receives the same test protocol.

Requirement Confirmation

Record the approved product, color, size, assortment, packaging and labeling requirements.

Sample Review

Compare samples with the buyer brief and retain approved references for repeat orders.

Appearance Check

Review major color differences, obvious defects, contamination, breakage and inconsistent assortments.

Quantity Verification

Confirm unit counts, pack ratios, carton quantities and order reconciliation.

Packaging Review

Check sealing, labels, barcodes, inner packing, cartons and buyer instructions.

Pre-shipment Review

Document final issues requiring correction before shipment handoff.

Testing Documentation

Quality checks are not the same as market certification.

Buyers should confirm whether small-parts warnings, labeling, ASTM F963, CPSIA, EN 71 or other destination-specific requirements apply to the exact product and intended use.

  • No blanket “all products certified” statement
  • Reports matched to exact SKU where available
  • Intended use and age grading reviewed separately
  • Destination requirements confirmed before ordering
  • Packaging and labeling checked as distinct requirements

Information needed for a documentation review

Product code or photo, material description, intended use, destination country, target age group where relevant, packaging format and any retailer-specific requirements.
