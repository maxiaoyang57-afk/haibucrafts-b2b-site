Buyer FAQ

Ocean sourcing questions

Can ocean charms and sequins be sourced together?

Yes. Send product codes and the packing plan so materials can be reviewed as separate or combined selling units.

Can I request a specific palette?

Yes. Provide color references and identify which shapes or finishes may vary.

Are original sea-animal designs possible?

Custom feasibility can be reviewed from an original brief defining animal, pose, dimensions, colors and finish.

Original and non-branded by design

Use generic motifs and original artwork. Do not request protected characters, logos, team marks or copied patterns.
