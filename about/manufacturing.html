Working Process

From buyer brief to production release

01

Requirement review

We confirm the target product, quantity, dimensions, colors, mix ratio, packaging, destination market and delivery schedule.

02

Sample development

For custom projects, samples or digital references are reviewed before the bulk specification is approved.

03

Production control

Production follows the confirmed specification, with checks on appearance, dimensions, mix consistency and packing.

04

Final packing

Finished goods are packed according to the agreed retail, bulk or private-label configuration and prepared for shipment.
