Candy charms for slime sourcing questions

Can candy and mini food charms be ordered as a mixed assortment?

Mixed-SKU assortments can be reviewed when you provide selected product codes, target quantities or ratios, packing requirements and destination market.

What is the MOQ for candy charms for slime?

MOQ is confirmed by selected SKU, quantity, packing format and customization scope. There is no blanket MOQ published for every item.

Can custom packaging or private-label packs be reviewed?

Yes. Bag, jar, label, barcode, warning text and outer-carton requirements can be reviewed as part of the project brief.

Are candy and food-shaped charms edible?

No. These are decorative craft components and must not be represented or used as food.
