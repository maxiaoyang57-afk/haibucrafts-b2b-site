Responsible Positioning

Factory-backed sourcing support without overstating ownership.

The page uses wording such as “factory resources,” “production coordination,” “warehouse operations” and “packing support” until the exact ownership relationship is confirmed.

  • No unsupported “100% own factory” claim
  • No unverified monthly capacity number
  • No blanket certification statement
  • No guaranteed lead time without product context
  • No claim that every process is completed at one site

Recommended buyer inquiry details

Product category, product code or reference image, target quantity, packaging, destination country, required delivery date and any testing or labeling requirements.
