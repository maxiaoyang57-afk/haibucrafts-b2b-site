Content Principles

Useful guidance without unsupported guarantees.

Each guide separates operational checks from legal compliance, certification and laboratory testing. Exact requirements depend on the selected SKU, intended use, age grading and destination market.

  • No universal MOQ or lead-time promises
  • No blanket certification statements
  • No substitution of operational inspection for third-party testing
  • Product- and market-specific confirmation required
