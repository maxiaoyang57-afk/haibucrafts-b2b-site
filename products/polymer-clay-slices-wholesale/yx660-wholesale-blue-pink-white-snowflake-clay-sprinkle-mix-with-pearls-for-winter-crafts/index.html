Detailed Product Description

Developed for slime brands, tumbler makers, nail suppliers and winter craft wholesalers, this wholesale snowflake clay slice and pearl mix combines snowflakes with pearl and candy-sprinkle accents with a soft matte clay texture with pearl highlights. The blue, pink, white and customizable soft winter palettes palette makes it easy to build a coordinated soft winter snowflake collection range.
