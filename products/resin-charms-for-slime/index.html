How to choose and request a bulk quotation

Flatback cabochons
Request a rear-view image and confirm the bonding surface and dimensions for your application.
Dimensional miniatures or hanging charms
Confirm shape, weight and any hole or loop placement against the selected SKU; do not assume every charm has a flat back.
Bulk assortments
Specify quantities by SKU, piece count per pack, mix ratios and destination. Confirm MOQ, dispatch timing and sample requirements in the quotation.

Buyer FAQ

Common questions before requesting a quotation.

Can we combine several resin charm SKUs?

Mixed sourcing can be reviewed. Send the selected product codes, target quantities and packing requirements.

Can you support custom colors or themes?

Custom feasibility depends on design detail, finish, dimensions, quantity and packaging requirements.

Do you offer private-label packs?

Private-label options may include buyer artwork, labels, barcodes and customized pack formats, subject to confirmation.

Can you support product testing documentation?

Documentation must be reviewed for the exact SKU, intended use and destination market. No blanket certification claim applies to every item.

Need pricing for several resin charm codes?

Send the product codes, quantities, packaging requirements and destination country in one inquiry.
